# Project 01: E-Commerce Category Performance

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset to evaluate revenue performance, product category trends, seasonality, and customer purchasing behavior.

The objective is to identify the primary drivers of revenue, understand purchasing patterns, and uncover opportunities to improve merchandising strategy and customer lifetime value.

## Business Objectives

- Identify leading product categories and revenue drivers.
- Evaluate revenue trends and seasonal purchasing patterns.
- Examine customer purchasing behavior and revenue concentration.
- Identify opportunities to improve product performance and customer retention.
- Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## Tools & Methods

- SQL
- Power BI
- DAX
- Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Modeling
- KPI Development
- Revenue Analysis
- Category Performance Analysis
- Customer Behavior Analysis

## Dataset

- Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist
- Approximately 99,000 orders
- Customer, order, payment, product, and transaction data

## Analytical Approach

1. Prepared and cleaned the transactional data using SQL.
2. Joined relevant tables to analyze orders, products, customers, and revenue.
3. Developed key performance indicators to evaluate business performance.
4. Examined revenue by product category and over time.
5. Evaluated seasonal trends and customer purchasing patterns.
6. Used Power BI dashboards to communicate findings and business opportunities.

## Key Findings

- Revenue demonstrates meaningful seasonal variation, with particularly strong Q4 performance.
- Gift-oriented and lifestyle categories perform especially well during peak purchasing periods.
- Revenue is concentrated among a relatively small group of high-value customers.
- The customer base consists primarily of one-time purchasers, indicating a significant retention opportunity.
- Product categorization and data-quality limitations affect the completeness of some category-level analyses.

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen marketing and merchandising ahead of seasonal gifting periods.
- Improve product visibility and inventory planning for high-demand categories.
- Expand promising categories to support more consistent revenue throughout the year.
- Introduce personalized post-purchase communications and second-purchase incentives.
- Improve product taxonomy and data integrity to support more reliable business decisions.

## Business Takeaway

The analysis highlights the importance of balancing revenue generation with long-term customer development. Seasonal demand and high-value customers contribute meaningfully to performance, while the prevalence of one-time purchases presents an opportunity to improve customer lifetime value through retention, personalization, and stronger post-purchase engagement.
